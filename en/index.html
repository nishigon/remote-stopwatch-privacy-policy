This page explains how information is handled in Remote Stopwatch. The Japanese version is the authoritative version.

1. Scope

This policy applies to information handled in connection with Remote Stopwatch. Remote Stopwatch is a non-official measurement app that uses two or more smartphones to measure time between separated points such as a start point and a goal point. Devices assigned as START and STOP participants join the same measurement room, and the app corrects press timestamps and displays the result. It is intended for training, practice, events, and casual use rather than official competition timing.

2. Information not intentionally collected

The initial version is not intended to collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Location information
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Audio
  • Files stored on the device
  • Camera footage or video

If future features add goal-confirmation video correction or similar processing, this policy will be updated before release.

3. Information that may be handled

  • Room ID, Client ID, joinSecret, and temporary connection data similar to a session token
  • Device role information such as host, starter, stopper, and viewer
  • Optional labels such as device name, lane label, and runner name
  • START events, STOP events, and START/STOP press timestamps
  • Measurement results such as elapsedMillis, ranking, and history
  • RTT, offset, estimated error, and WebSocket connection status
  • Communication status, diagnostic logs, and test-log fields such as appPhase
  • Advertising identifiers or related data handled by ad SDKs when AdMob is enabled
  • Purchase state handled when Google Play Billing is enabled

Device name, lane label, and runner name are optional labels. They can be general labels such as "Lane 1", "Device A", or "Finish judge". If a personal name is entered, it may be shown to other devices in the same room or in copied/shared result text.

4. Purpose of use

  • Create, join, and maintain measurement rooms
  • Synchronize START and STOP events
  • Calculate corrected times
  • Display results and history, and create copy/share text
  • Display connection status and estimated error
  • Investigate issues and respond to inquiries
  • Determine feature restrictions if Free/Pro control or ad stock management is added in the future

5. Retention period

Room information and connection information are generally handled temporarily only to the extent needed to maintain the measurement room and display results. In-app history may be stored on the device. If the user sends logs such as Copy test log, they may be used for support and troubleshooting.

If long-term cloud history storage is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated.

6. Third-party services, ads, and billing

Personal information is not sold. Infrastructure services such as Cloudflare may be used for server communication.

If ads are enabled, Google AdMob may be used, and Google may handle ad identifiers or related data. Ads may be mocked or disabled during initial testing. The Free version may show banner ads or rewarded ads, while the Pro version is intended to be ad-free.

If billing is enabled, Google Play Billing may be used, and Google may handle purchase status. Pro features may in the future include ad-free use, unlimited measurement, multiple STOP support, and stronger history storage. Payment card information is not directly obtained by the developer.

7. Use by children

This app is not primarily intended for children under 13. If a minor uses the app, use should be decided by a parent or responsible guardian.

8. Security

Public releases are intended to use secure communication methods such as HTTPS and WSS. Development-only local connections such as debugLocal are not intended for public release. Signing keys, secrets, and tokens are not included in the public repository.

9. Revisions

This policy may be revised as needed. The latest version will be published on this page.
