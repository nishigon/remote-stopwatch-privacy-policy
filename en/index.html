Remote Stopwatch Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 30, 2026

Remote Stopwatch is a non-official stopwatch app for training, practice, events, and casual use. It is designed to work without a user account. We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, audio, files on your device, or camera video.

If future features add video-based finish verification or any other new processing, we will update this policy before the feature is released.

Information used by the app

To provide room-based stopwatch features, the app may transmit operational data such as:

  • Room ID and join information
  • Client-generated identifiers
  • joinSecret and temporary session-token-like connection data
  • Device role, such as host, starter, stopper, or viewer
  • Optional device name or lane label and runner name
  • START and STOP events
  • Press timestamps for START and STOP
  • elapsedMillis and other measurement results
  • Ranking, history, RTT, offset, estimated error, and WebSocket connection status
  • Communication status, diagnostic logs, and test-log fields such as appPhase
  • Ad-related identifiers if AdMob is enabled
  • Purchase state if Google Play Billing is enabled

Optional labels such as device name, lane label, and runner name can be entered with general-purpose terms like "Lane 1", "Device A", or "Finish judge". If you enter a personal name, that name may be visible to other devices in the same room or in shared result text.

Purpose of use

  • Create and join measurement rooms
  • Synchronize START and STOP events
  • Calculate corrected times
  • Show results and history
  • Prepare copy/share text for results
  • Show connection quality, error estimates, and diagnostics
  • Manage feature availability if Free/Pro limits or ad stock controls are added later

Storage and retention

Room information and connection data are handled temporarily, only as needed to maintain the current room and display results. In-app history may be stored on the device. If you send logs such as Copy test log, we may use them for support or troubleshooting.

Third-party services, ads, and billing

Some builds may use Cloudflare or other infrastructure services for server communication. If ads are enabled, we may use Google AdMob and Google may process ad identifiers or related signals. If billing is enabled, we may use Google Play Billing and Google may process purchase state. We do not directly receive payment card details.

Children's privacy

The app is not intended primarily for children under 13. If a minor uses it, we expect a parent or guardian to decide whether that use is appropriate.

Security

We intend to use secure communication methods such as HTTPS and WSS for production services. Development-only local connections are not meant for public release. Secret keys, tokens, and signing material are not stored in this repository.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact nishigon.app.support@gmail.com.
