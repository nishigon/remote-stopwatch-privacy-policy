This page explains how information is handled in Remote Stopwatch. The Japanese version is the authoritative version.

Remote Stopwatch (the “App”) connects multiple devices to the same timing room and displays elapsed time from START and STOP/GOAL actions. This policy explains how information is handled by the Android and iOS apps and the server used by the App. The App is not intended for official competition timing.

1. Information handled

To operate timing rooms, the App handles a room ID, a client ID for each device, temporary connection and authentication information needed to join, device roles and connection status, START and STOP/GOAL action times, communication timing and correction values used for synchronization, and timing results. If you enter optional device, lane, or runner labels, the App handles those labels too. A personal name you enter may appear on other devices in the same room or in text you choose to share. Network connections also provide the server operator with information needed for communication, such as an IP address.

Timing history is stored on the device and can be viewed and deleted in the App. Older local history may be cleared when the Free history limit applies. If you choose to copy or share a result, the text goes to the destination you select. The App does not automatically send your complete history to an external sharing destination.

On iOS, the camera is used to read room invitation QR codes. The App has no feature that sends camera footage or photos to the timing server. Room join information contained in a QR code is used to join a room. The App also has no feature that automatically sends contacts, microphone audio, or device photos or files to the timing server. The App itself does not request precise GPS location. Advertising services may, however, handle approximate location inferred from an IP address.

2. Purchases, ads, and consent

TEAM extension purchases and management use Google Play Billing on Android and Apple StoreKit on iOS. To check purchase status and expiry and verify TEAM access for a room, the Android app sends purchase proof and the iOS app sends a signed, verified transaction to the App's server. The developer does not directly obtain payment card numbers through the App. Purchases, cancellations, and refunds are handled through the relevant store.

Free screens eligible for ads may show Google AdMob banner or rewarded ads. In the current Android and iOS apps, ad requests from a purchasing device are suppressed while its own active TEAM entitlement is verified. Ads on another device in the same room depend on that device's own entitlement and ad settings.

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform SDK for ads and consent management. Depending on configuration, region, consent, device restrictions, and ad use, these SDKs may handle IP address, approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, ad and app interaction data, and crash or performance diagnostics. Google's notices and the consent screen presented in the App describe the applicable collection and use. You can change advertising choices through a consent screen when available and through device privacy settings. If required ad consent is unavailable, the types of ads available may change or ads may not be shown.

3. Purposes and recipients

Information is used to create, join, and maintain rooms; synchronize device times; display results and history; verify TEAM access and usage limits; display ads and manage consent; investigate connection problems and defects; and respond to inquiries. The App uses Cloudflare server infrastructure for timing communications, Google for ads and consent management, Google Play for Android purchases, and Apple for iOS purchases. When you use a sharing feature, the text also goes to the destination you select. The developer does not sell user information.

4. Storage and deletion

Local timing history may remain on the device until you delete it in the App or the App's data is removed. Clearing associated with Free storage limits is described in the App. The server may store information needed to operate rooms, verify entitlements, and investigate problems. Expiration or closure of a room does not mean that stored data and operational logs are deleted at the same time. Please contact us below with questions about storage or deletion.

5. Your choices and security

You can use generic labels instead of entering runner or device names. You can delete local history, use advertising consent choices when shown, use OS privacy settings, and manage subscriptions in the relevant store. If you do not allow camera access, you can use the available room-code entry method instead of scanning a QR code. Public versions of the App use HTTPS/WSS for timing communications. The App is designed not to include connection secrets or purchase proof in public text or shareable diagnostic logs.

6. Children

The App is not primarily intended for children under 13. A parent or responsible adult should decide whether a minor uses it.

7. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. For questions about data handling or deletion, contact nishigon.app.support@gmail.com.
